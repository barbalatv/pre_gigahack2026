» About the hackathon

What is a
Hackathon?

A hackathon is an intense, fast-paced innovation marathon where tech talents, researchers, and entrepreneurs team up to solve real-world challenges using cutting-edge technologies.

 

It’s about proactive problem-solving, rapid prototyping, and pushing the boundaries of innovation in AI, FinTech, EnergyTech, and more…

Who can join?

/01
Tech Gurus & Innovators
Seasoned IT professionals, engineers, and experts eager to push the boundaries of deep tech. If you’re ready to apply your knowledge, experiment with emerging technologies, and craft groundbreaking solutions, this is your space!

Possibilities

Technovator is a dynamic platform dedicated to developing talent, fostering entrepreneurship, and driving innovation in Moldova’s tech ecosystem. Deeply connected to a thriving network of startups, IT companies, and industry leaders, we empower individuals and businesses to grow and innovate.

 

Our initiatives welcome proactive and curious minds - from graduating students and entrepreneurs to researchers, SMEs, and innovation enthusiasts. Whether you’re looking to sharpen your skills, scale your business, or explore cutting-edge technologies, you’ll find opportunities through our programs, including the Deeptech Academy, Deeptech Mentorship Program, Business Innovation Summit, SALTO, Technovator Labs and the Science Café.
